Bidoro

Trust is the product

16 October 2026 · David Ochuko Yaya

People already trade on WhatsApp. They already send photos of goods and account numbers in the same chat. The failure is not discovery. The failure is the moment money moves and nobody can unwind it.

Bidoro exists for that moment. Verified sellers. A conversation tied to an order. Buyer protection. Payments that sit with a licensed partner instead of in a stranger’s personal account. Reviews that mean a completed deal, not a drive-by star on a product page.

What I actually own

As technical co-founder I am not decorating a storefront. I am responsible for the architecture that makes those promises enforceable: identity, order state, escrow timing, what an admin can see, and what they must not. A button that says “protected” while the ledger does something else is worse than no button.

The same standard shows up next door at Ojarun. An agent in a real market, a freshness check, a rider, a doorstep. Software is the coordination layer. Trust is still a person who picked the tomatoes.

Why I keep both

Spendprofile asks whether a number is true. Bidoro asks whether a trade is safe. Ojarun asks whether the market can come to you without you standing in it. Different customers. One job: do not ship a confident lie.
